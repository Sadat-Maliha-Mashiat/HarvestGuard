Our solution helps reduce food loss by providing real-time weather updates, crop health monitoring, and efficient storage planning.

Why HarvestGuard is Different

Hyper-Local Weather Engine

Uses location-based forecasts for specific villages and unions (where available), instead of generic district-level information.

Smart Risk Scoring

Combines crop type, harvest date, storage method, and upcoming weather to estimate spoilage risk for each stored lot.

Actionable, Farmer-Friendly Advice

Turns complex data into simple steps like “Harvest within 2 days”, “Extend drying by 1 day”, or “Move sacks to a drier place”.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is HarvestGuard for?

Small and medium farmers, local aggregators, cooperatives, NGOs and agri-startups working to reduce post-harvest loss.

What data does HarvestGuard use?

Farm location, crop type, harvest dates, storage details and weather data from trusted forecast providers.

How will I receive alerts?

Alerts can be delivered through the web app, and can be extended to SMS or messaging apps depending on connectivity and project setup.
